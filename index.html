Integrally Bladed Fan (IBF) Polishing Template

Professional · Pratt & Whitney

Process Improvement

Problem

Manual polishing required operator judgement to identify the specific section of the blade requiring touch-up.

Improve consistency and accelerated manual polishing operations by approximately 25% on the trailing edge and 14% on the leading edge.
