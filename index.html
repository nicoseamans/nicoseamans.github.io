Airfoil Lab Fixture

Professional, Pratt & Whitney
Problem
Blades needed to be securely and consistently fastened to enable fast, repeatable CMM measurements of the airfoil.
Solution
Designed a magnetic bridge fixture with 7 points of contact, secured by a thumb screw to maintain consistent pressure and location across measurements, per GD&T inspection requirements.
Result
Reduced setup-induced measurement error and improved inspection speed and accuracy by about 10%.

Indoor Golf Simulator

Passion project
Problem
Create an indoor retractable golf simulator on a limited budget.
Solution
Designed and built a motorized retractable impact screen.
Drive train
12 V reversible worm-gear motor driving a 15T to 32T #25 roller chain reduction (about 2.1:1), powered by a 12 V, 5 A supply and switched by a 50 m wireless forward/reverse relay remote.
