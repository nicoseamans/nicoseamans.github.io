01 / SELECTED WORK

Engineering projects

Airfoil Lab Fixture

Professional · Pratt & Whitney
Manufacturing

Problem: Components required secure and repeatable positioning for dimensional inspection.

Solution: Designed a magnetic bridge-style fixture with controlled contact points and a thumb-screw mechanism to establish consistent positioning.

Result: Reduced setup-induced measurement variation and improved inspection speed and accuracy by approximately 10%.

Indoor Golf Simulator

Personal Engineering Project
Mechanical Design

Problem: Build a retractable indoor golf simulator that is compact, inexpensive, and simple to operate.

Solution: Developed a motorized retractable impact-screen system using a geared motor and roller-chain reduction.

Drive: 12 V reversible worm-gear motor with a 15-tooth to 32-tooth #25 chain reduction and wireless control.

Dog Ball Launcher

Academic · Machine Design
Design

Problem: Design an indoor tennis-ball launcher capable of operating safely inside a narrow hallway.

Solution: Developed a dual-flywheel launching mechanism, progressing from initial concept through CAD, component selection, and detailed part drawings.

Design: The system used two independently driven flywheels to accelerate the ball before launching it at a controlled angle.

CMM Refurbishment

In Progress · UNB
Metrology

Problem: The UNB machine shop has an older coordinate measuring machine that has been out of service.

Solution: Working toward troubleshooting and refurbishing the machine and developing dimensional inspection programs.

Progress: Project scope is being defined with the Department of Mechanical Engineering.

Bicycle to E-Bike Conversion Kit

Senior Design · In Progress
Product Design

Problem: Develop an affordable conversion system for riders wanting electric assistance without replacing their existing bicycle.

Solution: Designing a low-cost, easy-install 500 W motor system mounted to a rear rack and driving the rear tire by direct contact.

Design: Battery and controller integrated into the rear rack without requiring permanent modification to the bicycle.
